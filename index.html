🧩 Puzzle Resolver

Photo of the box picture

Photograph the finished picture on the box lid. Then drag the four corners onto the corners of the picture itself. A sideways photo is fine: the app turns it to match your puzzle.

Puzzle grid

Columns and rows are estimated from the piece count and the picture's shape. Count the pieces along one edge to correct them if needed.

Photo of what is already built

Photograph the puzzle from straight above. Drag the corners to where the corners of the finished puzzle will be. The border is usually done first, so this is easy.

Filled cells

Green cells are detected as already placed. Tap a cell to toggle it. Your corrections are remembered.

Photo of the loose pieces

Spread the pieces face up on a plain cloth or board so they don't touch, and photograph them from above. For a big puzzle, take several photos, each of a different part of the cloth. Don't photograph the same pieces twice. It's fine if the floor, a chair or the box show at the edges.
